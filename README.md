The 160G plan is a practical middle ground. With 8 GB of RAM and 160 GB of storage, it can handle a more substantial web application, several containers, a database, and background jobs more comfortably than the entry-level plans.

That does not mean every workload will perform well automatically. CPU contention, application design, database configuration, and disk activity still matter. More RAM cannot fix inefficient software by itself.

### For high traffic or large deployments

The 320G and 480G standard plans provide 16 GB and 24 GB of RAM respectively. They are more suitable for larger databases, multiple applications, staging environments, or several independent services on one server.

At this level, it is worth comparing the standard plan with an e-commerce or SLA product. A cheaper VPS with enough resources may be the better choice if your main concern is capacity. A specialized plan may be worth the extra cost if route quality, redundancy, or contractual uptime matters more.
